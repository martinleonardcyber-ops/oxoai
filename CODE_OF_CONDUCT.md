# Code of conduct

OxoAI is built by and for people who want to keep control of their own tools. We want its
spaces (issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews) to be welcoming and useful to everyone,
whatever their experience, background or language.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Be precise: describe what you did and what you saw, so others can help.
- Welcome newcomers, and answer in the language of the person you are replying to when you
  can (English and French are both fine here).
- Accept that a proposal may be declined, and explain your own decisions when you decline one.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sharing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or spam.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules, and may block people who
repeat them. To report a problem, open an issue on https://github.com/martinleonardcyber-ops/oxoai/issues; reports are handled
confidentially.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
